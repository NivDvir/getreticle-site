Was der Bericht enthält

  • Der Dienst misst Textkontraste im ersten Bildschirm geprüfter Seiten. Er findet nicht alles.
  • Text über Bildern, Verläufen oder Überlagerungen wird als „Kontrast nicht sicher messbar“ ausgewiesen, nicht bewertet.
  • Dazu automatisierte Prüfungen der Seitenstruktur.

Accessibility check: text contrast and automated checks

I build an engine that finds and marks accessibility problems on websites and web applications, reading both the rendered screen and the page's structure.

It is automated, with nothing to install on your website, and with a manual check where needed.

Open the contact form Ordering niv@getreticle.com

What the report contains

  • The service measures text contrast on the first screen of the pages checked. It does not find everything.
  • Text over images, gradients or overlays is shown as "Contrast not reliably measurable", not rated.
  • Plus automated checks of the page structure.
  • Every contrast finding is marked on the screenshot. The report is not a conformity statement.

Delivery

As a report in the browser and as a PDF.
